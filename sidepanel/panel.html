Features

Switch off what you don't use to declutter the panel. Turning a feature off hides it and stops its schedules; your data is kept, and turning it back on restores everything.

Off: Hides the Focus button, banner and F shortcut. A running session finishes normally.
Off: Hides AI status, Smart group, summaries, AI suggestions and AI settings. Keys stay saved.
Off: Hides the natural-language command bar (it also needs AI on).
Off: Hides the search button and Ctrl+K search.
Off: Hides the Windows view and window consolidation.
Off: Hides the Stash view and Stash buttons, and stops auto-stash. Stashes stay restorable.
Off: Hides the Sessions view and stops auto-save. Saved sessions are kept.
Off: Hides the Duplicates tab and badge and stops the background duplicate scan.
Off: Stops auto-save, auto-kebab and auto-stash schedules and hides their settings.
Off: Stops bookmark snapshots (manual and on stash) and hides bookmark settings.
Off: Stops Drive sync and Drive retention and hides Drive buttons and settings. Nothing is removed from Drive.

Setup Guide

Requirements:

  • Chrome 127+ (ideally 138+)
  • 22GB free disk space
  • GPU with 4GB+ VRAM

Steps:

  1. Go to chrome://flags
  2. Enable "Prompt API for Gemini Nano"
  3. Enable "Enables optimization guide on device"
  4. Restart Chrome
  5. Go to chrome://components
  6. Find "Optimization Guide On Device Model"
  7. Click "Check for update" (downloads ~2GB)

After setup, return here and test the connection.
